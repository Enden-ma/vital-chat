* The Courier Service: Short-term memories collected throughout the day in the hippocampus are systematically transported up into the cortex for permanent long-term storage, emptying the short-term memory cache for the next day.

* The Filing System: "Nerves that fire together, wire together." The brain categorizes and files the memories uploaded during NREM, grouping positive emotions with positive, and negative with negative. This system creates emotional stability.
