When transitioning away from refined salt, unrefined sea salts, such as Celtic sea salt, present a vastly superior alternative. These naturally evaporated salts retain a broad spectrum of trace minerals and do not trigger the same toxic, dehydrating response in the body. They are highly beneficial for basic mineral replenishment. Furthermore, because they are formed through relatively rapid surface evaporation, their minerals remain in a coarse state and lack a highly ordered geometric structure.
